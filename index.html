Mandatory Terms & Policy Gateway

Please review and accept SplitSmart’s peer-to-peer settlement & privacy policies before accessing your account dashboard.

1. Peer-to-Peer Expense Splitting Agreement

SplitSmart enables registered members to calculate, track, and divide communal expenses across trips, shared housing, dining, and social events. By proceeding, you confirm that all entered expenses represent genuine shared transactions and that you are authorized by group members to record debts.

2. PayPal Direct Settlement & API Authorization

SplitSmart integrates directly with PayPal’s Smart Checkout APIs to facilitate instant peer-to-peer settlements. Whenever you click “Pay with PayPal” or “Settle Up”, SplitSmart dynamically loads the exact pre-calculated amount into the secure PayPal payment gateway with zero additional platform fees.

3. Sensitive Bank Data Protection & PIN Masking

In compliance with strict fintech security standards, all linked banking records, routing numbers, and PayPal account identifiers remain encrypted and visually masked by default. Sensitive financial data can only be revealed on your personal dashboard by entering your account password or PIN.

4. Foreign Exchange & Zero Markup Guarantee

When utilizing international travel split features or the live currency exchange calculator, conversion rates reflect real-time mid-market rates provided for transparent estimation. Settlements execute securely in your selected currency.

5. Anti-Fraud & Dispute Protection

All transactions settled through SplitSmart are backed by PayPal Buyer & Seller Protection mechanisms where applicable. Fraudulent activity or intentional misattribution of shared expenses will lead to immediate account suspension.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
How does the 1-Click PayPal Settlement work?
Whenever an expense is logged or split, SplitSmart calculates each person's exact share. When you click "Pay with PayPal" or "Settle Up", our smart checkout modal opens with the exact amount pre-filled (like $45.00). You just confirm your payment method without typing the amount!
Why are my bank details masked on the dashboard?
To protect your financial privacy when checking the app in public or on mobile devices, your account numbers and routing details are masked by default. Simply enter your password or PIN in the security gate widget to reveal them.
Are there transaction fees for peer-to-peer splitting?
SplitSmart charges $0.00 platform fees for peer-to-peer settlements. When paying friends and family via PayPal balance or linked bank accounts, domestic transfers are zero fee.
How does the AI Receipt Scanner parse taxes and tips?
Our optical character recognition (OCR) model parses individual receipt line items, detects subtotals, applies sales taxes, and distributes gratuity proportionately across all participating members.
